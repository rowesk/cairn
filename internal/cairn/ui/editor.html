Structure

# Heading
A section heading; ## and ### go smaller. Headings make the page’s contents.
- item
A list; 1. item numbers it
- [ ] task
A checklist item; [x] ticks it
> quote
A quotation
---
A rule between sections

Tables

| Plan  | Price |
| ----- | ----: |
| Basic |    £5 |

The second line sets the columns; a colon on the right aligns numbers.

Callouts

> [!NOTE]
> Bring the booking reference.

Also [!TIP], [!IMPORTANT], [!WARNING] and [!CAUTION]. [!VERDICT] and [!SUMMARY] set a conclusion larger.
